The fluency journey

From your first letter to fluent recitation

Whatever stage you start at, every student moves through the same four stages. Your teacher adjusts the pace. The destination does not change.

  1. 01 Month 1–2

    Foundations

    Arabic letters, correct articulation points (makhraj), and the essential rules of tajweed.

  2. 02 Month 3–4

    Building fluency

    Connected reading of longer passages, applying tajweed naturally instead of letter by letter.

  3. 03 Month 5

    Refining your flow

    Full surahs recited at a natural pace, with real confidence and a more measured tone.

  4. 04 Month 6

    Assessment & certificate

    A final recitation assessment, followed by your official certificate of completion.

Built for every learner

Every student is taught to the same standard, from wherever they begin.

Age and prior knowledge shape the pace of instruction, not the standard of it. Every student, regardless of starting point, is held to the same rigour.

Children

Structured, closely supervised instruction designed to build correct habits early and hold a young learner's attention.

Adult beginners

No prior knowledge is assumed. A significant number of students join without knowing the Arabic alphabet.

Intermediate reciters

Corrective coaching aimed at tajweed accuracy, articulation, and consistency, not simply repetition.

Memorization students

A dedicated Hifz track governed by disciplined revision, paced to what a student's schedule can sustain.
